The short version: you don't need an account, we don't use tracking cookies or ads, and your learning progress stays in your own browser.

## Who we are

The Programmer Bro (theprogrammerbro.in) is run by Mayank Raj Parmar in India. For anything about this policy or your data, email <Email />.

## What we collect

**Nothing that identifies you, unless you email us.** In detail:

- **No accounts.** You can read every lesson, take every quiz and read all the news without signing up.
- **No tracking cookies, no analytics and no ads.** We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracker. Our fonts are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or anyone else.
- **Server logs.** Like every website, our host, Vercel, processes technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so it can deliver the site and protect it from attacks. We don't use these logs to identify you. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).
- **Emails you send us.** If you write to us, we see your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

## What's saved in your browser

Two things are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They're never sent to us.

- Your choice of light or dark theme.
- Your quiz results: which lessons you've finished and your best score, so we can show your progress and what to read next.

To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Sign-in for the editor

Only the site's editor can sign in, at `/admin`, to approve news stories. That sign-in uses Supabase, which sets cookies for the editor alone. Visitors are never asked to sign in. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## Links to other websites

Lessons, news stories and resources link to other sites. When you open one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, and raise a complaint. Since we don't collect personal data unless you email us, there will usually be nothing to find, but we'll always answer. Email <Email />, and we'll reply within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy

If we add accounts, analytics or ads, we'll update this page before they go live and change the date at the top.
