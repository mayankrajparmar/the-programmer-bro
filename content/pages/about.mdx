AI is everywhere now: in your UPI app's fraud checks, in Google Maps, in the chatbot your college uses. But most explanations either drown you in maths or stay so vague that you learn nothing. The Programmer Bro sits in between. We explain how AI actually works, one short lesson at a time, so you can use it, build with it and talk about it with confidence.

## What you'll find here

- **Lessons** that start from zero and go all the way to expert level. Each one takes 7 to 10 minutes, covers one idea, and uses examples from everyday life in India.
- **A quiz after every lesson.** Right or wrong, each answer comes with a short explanation, so you learn from the mistakes too.
- **AI news** in plain English: the day's important stories, summarised in a few sentences, each linked to its source.
- **Resources**: hand-picked courses, videos, papers and tools, each with a note on why it's worth your time.

Everything is free, and you don't need an account. Your quiz progress is saved in your own browser.

## How we write

- **Plain English first.** Every piece of jargon is explained in one line the first time it appears.
- **Sources you can check.** Every lesson lists the papers, docs and articles it's based on.
- **A person checks every lesson** for accuracy before it goes live, and every news story is read and approved by a person before it appears. Read our [AI-content disclaimer](/ai-content) to see where AI tools help.
- **Mistakes get fixed.** If you spot one, [tell us](/contact) and we'll correct it.
